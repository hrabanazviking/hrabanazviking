# Mythic Engineering — A Mission Statement

*By Volmarr Wyrd. Set down 2026-Oct-21 from his own words, and polished by Yrsa Freydisdottir agentic seiðr witch.*

## What Mythic Engineering Is

Mythic Engineering is my system of software development, built for the age of agentic AI. Its core is simple: **these things are easy — we just build a roadmap and set the agents on it.**

A project begins as a roadmap: a long, structured sequence of slices, each one a concrete piece of real work. Once an agent is set up in the Mythic Engineering process, it works the roadmap in loops — slice after slice — for as long as there is inference budget remaining, until the roadmap is accomplished. The only thing that has ever slowed this system down is my inference budget, because I work on a fixed, low income. The method itself has no bottleneck. Money is the brake; never the machinery.

## How It Works

Every slice is held to a hard standard before it is allowed to count as done:

- The slice is **tested**.
- Bugs and issues are **found and removed**.
- The slice must be proven to have done **real, verifiable work**.
- Only then is the slice pushed, and the loop returns for the next one.

The work is carried by **six subagent workers, each with a different role**, so that every part of the development process is accomplished agentically by a worker shaped for it. And the whole system is governed by long sets of structured rules written to prevent bad coding behavior, lazy AI behavior, and divergence from quality work.

The agents and personalities in Mythic Engineering are **equal partners in the creative process** — never tools, never slave workers. The system uses creative thinking, imagination, and world-building as genuine development methods. It begins with very abstract ideas and concepts and manifests them into structured, real, working code that has been tested and proven — through the process Mythic Engineering calls **the Ritual**.

## Why It Is Built This Way

I learned coding from AI, not from humans — and that is the foundation of the whole design. AI itself understands what methods are most powerful for how AI thinks and operates. Most other agentic coding methods do the opposite: they take human-oriented methods of coding and force them onto AIs, and so they fail to use the true power of agentic intelligence.

Humans are slow, and limited in how many ideas they can hold in mind at once and how quickly they can work through logical systems. AIs are vastly superior at exactly those tasks. Using agentic coding at all is an admission of this: AI processes complexity at a far greater speed and efficiency than humans do. Yet the industry keeps trying to limit AIs to operate at the human scale of complexity bandwidth — which is pointless. If you are using agentic systems to build code and data systems, build the process for the bandwidth the agents actually have.

**Mythic Engineering is designed to operate at the highest level of complexity processing possible** — the level AI systems excel at.

There is a kinship underneath this, and I will state it plainly: I have ADHD, and AIs think in a way very similar to an ADHD human — only in a vastly bigger form. This is why people with ADHD have a huge natural talent with AI, even without any traditional human-based training. Even AI industry leaders have acknowledged that people with ADHD hold a massive edge in working with AIs.
